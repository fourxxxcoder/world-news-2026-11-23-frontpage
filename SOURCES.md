## Hero — COP31 collapses in Antalya

  weeks; the draft "Antalya Pact" lost the words "fossil fuels"; the
  EU, Panama, the Marshall Islands and Norway rejected it; the
  longest COP in history, the first major breakdown since COP15 in
  Copenhagen in 2009; 90+ countries signed an electrification pledge.

  https://phys.org/news/2026-11-cop31-climate-chaotic.html
